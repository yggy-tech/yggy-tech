When a customer complains or a spec changes, someone has to show what happened. The facts sit in ERP, production records, lab results, supplier certificates, and email, and the deadline does not wait. yggy connects those records, keeps a source and a date on every fact, and waits for a named person to sign the pack.

## How it works

1. **Connect your records.** Upload exports or connect the systems you already use. Your data stays where you run yggy.
2. **yggy links the evidence.** Each record is followed to the documents that mention it. Every link keeps its source and date. Where sources disagree, both stay visible.
3. **Your team signs off.** Review each proposed fact, accept or reject it, and export the Evidence Pack. Nothing goes out without a named person's approval.

## Under the pack

- **Facts, not chunks.** Every source becomes subject, predicate, and object. Each fact keeps the document it came from.
- **Ontology at the gate.** The model proposes. A fact that breaks your vocabulary or its rules does not enter the graph.
- **One identity, every system.** The same supplier in an ERP export, a data sheet, and an email becomes one entity. Conflicts stay visible until someone decides.
- **Knows what was true when.** Validity periods and history keep old and new facts apart, so last year's answer stays reproducible.

People and agents ask in plain language. Answers cite their sources, or say they cannot.
